GroupPlaylist is a playlist view for foobar2000 64-bit (Default UI). It groups the tracks of a playlist, for example by album, and shows the album art and an information column beside each group. Disc labels such as "Disc 1" can split a group further.

A 64-bit alternative to SimPlaylist, written from scratch. Not affiliated with the author of SimPlaylist.

Features:
* Groups and labels made from title formatting patterns, with ready-made presets, or no grouping at all
* 23 built-in columns plus your own columns
* Star ratings and likes you click, and a play count column (ratings and play counts use the Playback Statistics component)
* Colour emoji in titles and tags; WebP album art
* Follows the Default UI colours, fonts and dark mode
* Remembers the grouping and scroll position of each playlist
* English and Korean interface

Add it with View > Layout > Enable layout editing mode, then Replace UI Element > Playlist Views > GroupPlaylist. Right-click a column title for the panel menu. Settings are in Preferences > Tools > GroupPlaylist.

Requires foobar2000 v2.0 or newer, 64-bit.
